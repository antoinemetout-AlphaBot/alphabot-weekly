================================================================================
INDICATEURS TECHNIQUES
================================================================================

Tous calculés SANS ta-lib (pandas/numpy seulement):

SMA 20/50:
  sma = closes.rolling(window=N).mean()

RSI 14:
  Calcul standard: Gains/Pertes sur 14 périodes
  RSI = 100 - (100 / (1 + RS))

Bollinger Bands (20, 2):
  Upper = SMA + 2×STD
  Middle = SMA
  Lower = SMA - 2×STD

MACD (12, 26, 9):
  MACD = EMA12 - EMA26
  Signal = EMA9(MACD)
  Histogram = MACD - Signal
